# Approved Ambiguity Handling

## Purpose

Permit sensible progress without speculative scope.

## Scope

Reversible decisions supported by repository evidence.

## Non-goals

Ambiguity does not authorize semantic invention.

## Operational impact

State material assumptions in the change summary.

## Failure modes

An assumption may silently become a requirement.

## Rationale

Judgment must remain available where process cannot decide.
