# Health Model

## Purpose

Separate life, readiness, depth, and purpose.

## Scope

`/health/live`, `/health/ready`, and `/health/deep`.

## Non-goals

Purpose degradation does not fail the API.

## Operational impact

Deep health validates eleven principles and one sentence package.

## Failure modes

Healthy transport may deliver unhealthy interpretation.

## Rationale

Architecture exceeds product surface area by design and should say so.
