# Runbook: Excessive Clarity

## Purpose

Respond when the product becomes easier to understand than its governance model anticipated.

## Scope

Documentation navigation and direct explanations.

## Non-goals

Do not reduce clarity in user-visible doctrine.

## Operational impact

None; open an architectural review if concern persists.

## Failure modes

Review may discover that clarity is desirable.

## Rationale

The joke belongs in the repository, not the page.
