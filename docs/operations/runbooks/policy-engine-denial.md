# Runbook: Policy Engine Denial

## Purpose

Explain why public content became private to everyone.

## Scope

Kill switch, classification, reason code, and audit metadata.

## Non-goals

Do not bypass policy in transport code.

## Operational impact

Disable the deliberately named kill switch only with approval.

## Failure modes

The boolean may be correct and the process surrounding it may not be.

## Rationale

Denial must be structured enough to survive a retrospective.
